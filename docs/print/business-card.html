Printing this card

Two 85 × 55 mm panels below — the standard German/EU business card size. Print on cardstock (250–350 gsm) and cut along the dashed guide.

  • Easiest: print the front only, single-sided — everything essential (name, role, company) is already there.
  • Double-sided: print front and back on separate cardstock sheets, cut both out, and glue them back-to-back.
  • Best quality: send this page (or a PDF export of it — Print → Save as PDF) to a local print shop and ask for duplex printing at 85×55 mm.
  • The QR code links to https://www.silicortex.de — test it with a phone camera before your print run.
